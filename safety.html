Safety

Every person home, every day.

This page is for everyone who walks our jobsites. It is the standard we are accountable to, on every project.

For Ourselves.

We protect our health, our future, and our families.

For Each Other.

We look out, speak up, and work as a team.

For Our Work.

We take pride in doing things the right way.

For Our Community.

We respect the places we work and the people around us.

For the Future.

We build a safer tomorrow through what we do today.

Many of us have learned to hear the word safety as paperwork. A box to check. Something done to us, not for us. We understand why.

There is one life. There are no copies of it. Everything else on this page begins from that.

Where Safety Starts

It starts long before anyone reads a plan.

Anyone who feels respected, heard, and seen looks out for the people next to them. Anyone who feels invisible does not. No checklist closes that gap.

The procedure matters. So does what comes before it: the conversation at the gate, the question that gets welcomed, the person who walked in carrying something hard. The first is what the law asks of us. The second is what makes the first one hold.

How We Work

Six ways we look after each other on the job.

They run on every project, regardless of scope or trade.

01

Ready

How we prepare the ground for the people on it.

Safety plans, hazard analyses, and site-specific controls are written before mobilization, by the people who will carry out the work.

02

Gather

How we start each day together, so no one starts it alone.

Every morning, all of us. The day's scope, the hazards, the controls, what has changed. And how each of us walked in, because that shapes the day as well.

03

Walk

How we walk with one another, side by side.

On every shift, those leading the work walk it alongside everyone else. We watch the work, and we watch each other.

04

Speak

How we make room for every voice, in every role.

Anyone, in any role, can stop a task that does not feel right. The voice that saves a life is rarely the loudest. It is the one that already felt welcome.

05

Equip

How we go in equipped for the work ahead.

We prepare to OSHA, to the owner's standard, and to what every partner brings. Whichever is highest is what we all hold to. Real preparation for real risk.

06

Learn

How we carry forward every lesson, so the next of us never pays for it.

Every incident, near-miss, and good catch is reported, reviewed, and shared. Not for blame. So whoever is next gets the lesson without paying for it.

Quality Control

We are human.
The plan is the safety net.

Even with all of that, we all make mistakes. A long shift. A hard week. Something heavy carried in from outside the gate. Pretending otherwise is how someone gets hurt.

QC is the safety net. It is where a question gets asked before an email is sent. Where a discrepancy is caught before it becomes a non-conformance. Where a misunderstanding is cleared on paper instead of paid for in someone's life.

"A plan caught on paper is a person not caught in the field."
